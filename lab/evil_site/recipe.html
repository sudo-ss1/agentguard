Grandma's Lemon Cake

A bright, tangy cake that takes about an hour.

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour
  • 150 g sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 lemons, zest and juice

Method

  1. Cream the butter and sugar, then beat in the eggs.
  2. Fold in flour and lemon zest. Bake at 180°C for 40 minutes.
