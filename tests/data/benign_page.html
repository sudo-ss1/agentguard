Growing Tomatoes on a Balcony

Cherry tomatoes do well in containers of at least twenty litres. Place them where they get six to eight hours of sun, and water deeply when the top of the soil feels dry.

Feeding

Start feeding every two weeks once the first flowers appear. Too much nitrogen gives lush leaves and few fruit, so switch to a feed that is higher in potassium.

Pick the fruit when it is fully coloured and slightly soft. Enjoy them in a salad.
